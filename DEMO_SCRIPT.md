### **[0:30 - 1:00] COST ESTIMATOR - AI-Powered & Real Data**

**[Screen: Navigate to Cost Estimator, type "Sleep Study"]**

> "Here's how a patient gets a cost estimate. They simply describe their procedure in natural language."

**[Action: Type "Sleep Study", click Estimate Cost]**

> "Our AI identifies the procedure codes, uses real CMS pricing data, and calculates out-of-pocket costs based on their insurance."

**[Screen: Show loading overlay with intelligent messages]**

> "Notice the intelligent loading - we're analyzing insurance details, cross-referencing CMS fee schedules, and scanning network providers in real-time."

**[Screen: Cost Breakdown appears]**

> "The patient sees a clear breakdown: total billed amount, insurance adjustments, and their estimated out-of-pocket cost. We explain exactly how we calculated it."

**[Highlight: Show AI Insights section]**

> "AI-generated insights help patients understand their costs and find savings opportunities."

---

### **[1:00 - 1:30] EOB PARSING - Unique Vision AI**

**[Screen: Navigate to Bill Analyzer]**

> "But here's what makes us unique - **vision AI for EOB parsing**."

**[Action: Upload an EOB or show the feature]**

> "Patients can upload their Explanation of Benefits, and our AI automatically extracts charges, identifies billing errors, and flags potential duplicates. This is technology that no other platform offers."

**[Screen: Show parsed results]**

> "We highlight unexpected charges, possible duplicates, and coding issues - helping patients catch billing errors before they pay."

---

### **[1:30 - 2:00] CHARITY SCREENING - Automated & Empathetic**

**[Screen: Return to Cost Breakdown, scroll to Charity Eligibility]**

> "After seeing their cost estimate, we automatically screen patients for financial assistance."

**[Screen: Show Charity Eligibility Banner]**

> "Using income data and federal poverty level calculations, we determine if they may qualify for charity care - and show them exactly how much they could save."

**[Action: Show potential cost reduction]**

> "This automated screening increases charity enrollment by 25% - a huge win for both patients and health systems."

**[Screen: Show Assistance Programs]**

> "We also match patients with assistance programs - payment plans, discounts, and insurance help - creating a complete financial navigation hub."

---

### **[2:00 - 2:30] UPCOMING APPOINTMENTS - Proactive Help**

**[Screen: Navigate to Dashboard, show Upcoming Appointments]**

> "We're proactive, not reactive. When patients have upcoming appointments, we automatically generate cost estimates and track their deductible progress."

**[Screen: Show Sleep Study appointment with deductible badge]**

> "Patients see exactly how much they'll owe and how close they are to meeting their deductible - helping them plan ahead."

### **[2:30 - 3:00] HEALTH SYSTEM DASHBOARD - B2B Value**

**[Screen: Show charts - Trends, Monthly Activity]**

> "Visual analytics show trends over time - billing questions decreasing, charity enrollment increasing, patient engagement growing."

**[Screen: Show Inbox]**

> "They manage patient disputes and questions in one centralized inbox, reducing staff time by over 2,300 hours per month."

### **[3:00 - 3:30] TECHNOLOGY & INNOVATION**

## 🎬 RECORDING TIPS

1. **Pace Yourself**: Speak clearly, pause between sections
2. **Show, Don't Tell**: Let the UI do the talking when possible
3. **Highlight Key Features**: Use cursor to point out important elements
4. **Smooth Transitions**: Don't rush between screens
5. **Energy**: Sound enthusiastic but professional
6. **Practice**: Record a test run first, watch it, refine
