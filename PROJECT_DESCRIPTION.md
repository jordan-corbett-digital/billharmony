# BillHarmony: AI-Powered Healthcare Financial Navigation Platform

## Overview

BillHarmony is an AI-powered financial navigation platform that helps patients understand their healthcare costs, catch billing errors, and access financial assistance—while helping health systems reduce billing questions, increase charity enrollment, and improve patient satisfaction.

Built for the Codefi Vibeathon, BillHarmony combines natural language processing, computer vision, and predictive analytics to transform how patients interact with healthcare billing.

## The Problem

- **$220 billion** in medical debt affects millions of Americans
- Health systems waste **thousands of hours** monthly on billing questions
- **28% of patients** who qualify for charity care never apply
- CMS price transparency rules create compliance pressure
- Patients receive surprise bills with no context or support

## The Solution

BillHarmony provides a complete financial navigation experience powered by real AI—not chatbots, but actual machine learning that understands medical billing, insurance policies, and patient needs.

### For Patients

**AI Cost Estimator**
- Natural language input: "CT scan of my chest" → instant cost breakdown
- Real CMS pricing data and regional benchmarks
- Personalized calculations based on exact insurance plan
- AI-generated insights and money-saving tips

**Bill Analysis & EOB Parsing**
- Upload any medical bill or Explanation of Benefits
- Computer vision AI extracts and analyzes billing data in real-time
- Automatically flags duplicate charges, coding errors, and unexpected adjustments
- Explains issues in plain English

**Financial Assistance Screening**
- Automated eligibility screening against federal poverty guidelines
- Real-time charity care qualification
- Payment plan recommendations
- Assistance program matching

### For Health Systems

**ROI Dashboard**
- 40% reduction in billing questions
- 28% increase in charity enrollment
- 2,300+ hours saved monthly
- Centralized patient issue management

**B2B2C Deployment Model**
- White-labeled platform deployed by health systems
- Seamless integration with existing systems
- Branded patient experience builds trust

## Technical Innovation

### AI Capabilities

**Natural Language Processing**
- Converts patient queries into CPT codes
- Understands medical terminology and context
- Handles ambiguous requests with intelligent clarification

**Computer Vision**
- Parses complex EOB documents in real-time
- Extracts structured data from unstructured documents
- Identifies billing anomalies automatically

**Predictive Analytics**
- Screens patients for financial assistance eligibility
- Recommends assistance programs based on patient profile
- Predicts cost outcomes based on insurance and procedure data

### Data Sources

- **CMS Physician Fee Schedule** - Real federal pricing data
- **Hospital Machine-Readable Files** - Price transparency data
- **EOB Data** - Actual insurance outcomes for accuracy
- **Regional Benchmarks** - Location-specific pricing

### Technology Stack

- **Frontend:** React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS
- **Build Tool:** Vite
- **AI Integration:** OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini
- **Data Visualization:** Recharts
